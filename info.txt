name = Clamber: Climbing, Ladders & Mantling
author = AgeOfAlgorithms and Claude
description = Ladders, climbable walls and mantling on any map, in single player and multiplayer: every player in the session climbs, and everyone sees everyone climb (the host turns it on under Global mods). Walk into a ladder (or press interact at it): forward / back climb, left / right step off, jump jumps off, crouch lets go; at the top you climb over onto the floor (the hint at the bottom of the screen shows your own keys). Vines, nets, trellises and rock faces tagged climbable: move over them in any direction, over the top at the edge. Walk or jump into a ledge up to chest height to pull yourself up. Place a ladder from the spawn menu (Climbing > Ladder 3 m). For map makers: <location tags="ladder=<length>[,<lean>]"> at the foot of the rungs (its forward = the way the climber faces; lean = degrees from vertical, the top away from the climber), or tag any body or shape ladder; tag anything climbable (optional value: speed in m/s), or <location tags="climbable=<w>,<h>"> for an invisible patch on a wall (pos = its bottom centre, forward into the wall). Full API for maps and mods: API.md in the mod folder (and its GitHub repo). Game modes with their own movement: SetBool("climbing.off", true) on the host; climbing.nomantle = no free mantling; climbing.block.<player> = that player can't climb; climbing.on.<player> is true while a player climbs; climbing.present is true while this mod runs (synced). climbing.reach.<player> = that player's mantle reach in metres (a taller body; no reach cap); climbing.raymask = collision layers all of Climbing's rays skip (set with sync).
tags = Gameplay
version = 2
